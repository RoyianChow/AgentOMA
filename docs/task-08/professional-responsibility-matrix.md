## 4. Reading the responsibility matrix

The following **joined tables** use action IDs A01–A25. Read the responsibility, evidence and status tables together: no cell independently authorizes an action.

- **Responsible (R):** proposed performer. **Accountable (A):** professional or operational owner for that specific action, not a software account. **Consulted (C)** and **Informed (I)** are minimum-necessary workflow roles, not automatic data recipients or implemented messages.
- **P** = an actual authenticated pharmacist with separately verified current authority, assignment and exact action scope. **T** = technician contribution only under an explicitly approved technical scope. **O** = authorized pharmacy operations/staff function with an approved existing-role mapping; this mapping is PENDING, not a new role. **DM** = verified Designated Manager accountability function, presently NOT VERIFIED. **Patient/agent** always means a separately authorized actor for the subject.
- P is the conservative professional decision boundary from Task 08's planning table, not a claim that every listed action is universally pharmacist-exclusive under Ontario law. Any alternate registrant, trainee, technical scope, supervision or delegation requires exact current professional/legal review; unknown scope denies the affected action.
- DM oversees procedures/staffing/vendor accountability; the actual decision-maker remains accountable for their own professional decision. Consultation cannot substitute for the required decision, and delegation never means sharing or impersonating a professional session.

## 5. Non-transferable authority and stop conditions

1. **Current clinical permissions stay in their lane.** Do not reuse `ASSESSING_ROLES` or `RECORDING_ROLES` as a fulfilment allowlist, reinterpret `rx_issued` as dispensed/released, or use a recorded supervisor as the decision-maker for a trainee's Task 08 action. Existing assessment/as-of-right behavior remains unchanged.
2. **Professional identity stays personal and current.** P must be the actual authenticated, assigned and authorized registrant. DM accountability, admin status, support access, tenant membership or a generic pharmacy actor cannot stand in for that person. No impersonation or technician-to-pharmacist promotion. Unknown roles or unproven action scope fail closed.
3. **Choice is not tenancy.** Patient/agent choice must be neutral and reversible but cannot change server-only `PHARMACY_ID`. T08-D13/D14 remain unresolved; no mandatory AgentRx pharmacy, tenant selector or PHI expansion of public intake is approved.
4. **Coordination is not professional practice.** AgentRx may validate that an authorized professional supplied a supported decision with required evidence.
