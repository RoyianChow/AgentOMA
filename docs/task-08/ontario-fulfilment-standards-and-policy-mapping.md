## 3. Specific interpretation and conflict notes

### Operating Internet Sites Policy

Task 08 says the OCP Operating Internet Sites Policy is under review as of the task update and requires its implications to be resolved before production. **That repository statement is preserved. Its current external status is NOT VERIFIED.** No newly confirmed review date, final policy, supersession, accreditation decision or interpretation is asserted here. Production remains blocked even if an implementation could satisfy a historical text. See M01 and T08-D15.

### Delivery custody, proof, and retention

Task 08 records the distinction between pharmacy-to-courier custody and patient/patient-agent receipt and requires field-level retention review. Those are controlling design boundaries for this task. The exact current external signature/exception wording and retention periods have not been checked. Do not hardcode a signature waiver, infer a courier is an agent, apply one retention period to every record, or describe a webhook as professional acceptance of receipt. See M12, M13 and M17.

### Canadian hosting and privacy

The repository documents Canadian-region Postgres as its chosen posture. This does not establish every compute, logging, storage, vendor, backup or support-access location. `docs/architecture/SYSTEM-DESIGN-REVIEW.md` contains broader PHIPA-hosting language than Task 08 permits. This matrix does **not** repeat that language as law: **PHIPA must not be described here as universally requiring Canadian hosting.** Applicable legal requirements, contractual requirements and the project's stricter choices require separate documented review. No external legal conclusion is made (M19).

### Source precedence and approval

AGENTS.md preserves the binding minor-ailment regulatory PDF over conflicting local commentary. Workstream A does not reopen that source, derive billing values, extend its interpretation to dispensing, or override it. For new Task 08 topics, legal/professional reviewers must reconcile legislation, OCP standards/policies/guidance, payer terms, pharmacy procedures and vendor contracts. Source availability or a technical test PASS is never approval.
